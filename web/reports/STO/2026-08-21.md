# PART A — BUFFETT VALUE FRAMEWORK

## 1. Circle of Competence

**Boundary.** Oil and gas exploration and production sits at the edge of my circle of competence. The upstream business model is conceptually simple — find hydrocarbons, extract them, sell at market prices — but the commodity-price dependency, reserve depletion dynamics, and capital-intensity make durable competitive advantage exceptionally rare. Buffett has historically avoided pure E&P companies (his energy bets: regulated utilities like BHE, and integrated majors like Chevron/Oxy with downstream buffers). Santos is a pure-play upstream producer with no refining or marketing moat. I can analyse the financials and assets, but I cannot predict oil/gas prices or reserve economics with high confidence over a 10-year horizon. **Proceed with caution; this is a "watch" not a "buy" candidate.**

## 2. Key Assumptions (3–5)

The thesis rests on these explicit assumptions, each verifiable quarterly:

1. **Brent crude averages USD 70–80/bbl over the next 5 years** — below the USD 85+ needed to sustain current capex and dividends without further debt accumulation. (Check: futures curve, IEA/WEO scenarios)
2. **Pikka Phase 1 (Alaska) delivers ~80 kbpd plateau by 2027 on budget** — the single largest growth driver; any delay or cost overrun impairs the growth thesis. (Check: half-year operational updates)
3. **LNG netback prices (JCC-linked) remain above USD 10/MMBtu** — supporting Gladstone/Darwin LNG economics; a reversion to USD 8/MMBtu compresses margins severely. (Check: JKM spot, contract roll-offs)
4. **No material carbon price/regulatory impairment in Australia before 2030** — a Safeguard Mechanism tightening or carbon border adjustment would disproportionately hit Santos's high-CO2 assets (Bayu-Undan, Cooper Basin). (Check: policy announcements, Safeguard Mechanism reviews)
5. **Management maintains capital discipline** — resisting empire-building M&A; prioritising debt reduction and buybacks over low-return growth. (Check: capex guidance, acquisition announcements, buyback activity)
