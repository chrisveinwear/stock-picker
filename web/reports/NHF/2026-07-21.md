#### Valuation Reconciliation

The code fair value of AUD 5.20 diverges from the current price (AUD 7.47) by ~44% and from analyst consensus (AUD 7.73) by a similar margin — well above the 20% threshold requiring explicit reconciliation. Working through each authoritative input, none appear erroneous (EPS, share count, and price all tie out to the snapshot); the gap is driven by two **assumption** choices, not a model-structure or data problem:

1. **Graham Number is a poor fit for this business type.** It's an asset-based formula built for industrial/asset-heavy companies trading near book value. nib is a capital-light underwriter earning 17.9% ROE against an 8% cost of equity — a P/B of 3.27x is economically justified by that spread, not a sign of overvaluation. Including Graham as a co-equal, market-moving input (it's the single lowest of the three methods and directly sets the code median) systematically drags the blended "fair value" toward a figure a value investor wouldn't actually use for a financial-services underwriter. Treated instead as a floor/sanity-check (its traditional Graham-era role) rather than a co-equal method, it should be excluded from the blend.
2. **The owner-earnings multiple used the "average" quality tier (12.5x)**, which understates a narrow-moat, 17.9%-ROE, low-beta (0.08) defensive insurer with regulated, low-volatility demand. A "high" quality tier multiple (~15x, still within the model's stated 7–15x band) is more defensible: AUD 203.5m × 15 ÷ 488.9m shares ≈ **AUD 6.24/share**.
3. **The DCF's 12% stage-1 growth assumption is drawn from a single-year forward-EPS delta** (0.40 → 0.47, +17.5%), not from the business's demonstrated trend. The financial history shows a 3-year revenue CAGR (FY22→FY25) of ~9.4% — a more defensible steady-state growth assumption for a mature, regulated health insurer. Because the 8.0% discount rate sits so close to the 12% stage-1 growth rate, the DCF is highly sensitive to this input (the code model's own warning about a 40% sensitivity-grid swing corroborates this). Re-running with an 8–9% stage-1 growth assumption (in place of 12%) pulls the DCF-only value down from AUD 10.36 toward roughly the **AUD 7.50–8.50** area — this can't be recomputed exactly outside the model's code, but the direction and rough magnitude follow from the growth-vs-discount-rate spread compressing.

**Reconciled view:** blending an adjusted DCF (~AUD 8.05, using the more historically-grounded growth assumption) with the adjusted owner-earnings multiple (~AUD 6.24, high-quality tier) and treating Graham (AUD 4.52) as a floor rather than a blended input, gives a reconciled midpoint of roughly **AUD 7.50**, with a range of **AUD 6.40–8.60** once the sensitivity band and method spread are folded back in.
