## PART A — BUFFETT VALUE FRAMEWORK

### 1. Circle of Competence

**Assessment: Outside the circle — stop here; analysis continues only as an educational exercise.**

Centrex Limited was a junior phosphate mining company whose sole significant asset was the Ardmore Phosphate Rock Project near Julia Creek in northwest Queensland. The business model was: (1) hold a large in-ground phosphate resource; (2) attract capital and offtake partners to fund mine development; (3) eventually produce and sell phosphate rock (a commodity fertiliser input) into global agricultural markets. There is nothing complex about that description, yet the phrase "circle of competence" has two requirements — understanding the *business*, and understanding the *economics*. It is the second where junior miners fall down entirely.

The economics of a pre-revenue mining developer are dominated by: commodity price cycles that no one can reliably forecast; capital-cost blowouts that are endemic in Australian mining; sovereign/environmental approvals that routinely take years longer than planned; and a funding structure (perpetual equity dilution) that destroys per-share value even when the underlying asset is real. Buffett has explicitly avoided pre-revenue miners throughout his career. The CLAUDE.md investment philosophy for this portfolio confirms: resources are acceptable *only* for "wide-moat or lowest-cost producers" such as BHP, RIO, or FMG — not for junior developers whose intrinsic value is speculative.

**Decision: Fail — outside circle of competence. Analysis continues for completeness.**
