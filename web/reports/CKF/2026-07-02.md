## PART A — BUFFETT VALUE FRAMEWORK

### 1. Circle of Competence
**Inside.** The business is straightforward: Collins Foods is the largest KFC franchisee in Australia and a significant operator in Germany and the Netherlands, plus a smaller Sizzler footprint in Australia/Asia. Revenue comes from selling fried chicken and sides through company-owned stores. The model is capital-intensive (capex for remodels/new stores), operationally repetitive, and governed by a long-term franchise agreement with Yum! Brands. No complex financial engineering, obscure technology, or commodity price exposure beyond food inputs. **Verdict:** well within circle of competence.

### 2. Key Assumptions (3–5)
The investment thesis rests on these explicit assumptions, each verifiable in coming quarters:
1. **KFC Australia same-store sales growth (SSSG) sustains 2–3% annually** — driven by menu innovation, digital ordering, and population growth, not price increases alone.
2. **European operations (Germany/Netherlands) reach breakeven EBITDA by FY28** — the €31.1M Bavarian acquisition (Mar 2026) adds scale; integration risk is real but manageable given CKF's operating playbook.
3. **Net debt/EBITDA trends below 3.5x by FY27** — from ~5.3x currently (AUD 0.75bn net debt / AUD 141m FY26 EBITDA), requiring FCF generation > AUD 100m/yr and minimal M&A.
4. **Franchise agreement with Yum! Brands renews on comparable terms** — current agreement runs to 2030+ with renewal options; a material adverse change (higher royalties, mandated capex) would compress returns.
5. **No equity dilution** — the 118.1m share count holds; management has not raised equity since 2020 and FCF covers maintenance capex + interest.
