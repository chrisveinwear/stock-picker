## PART A — BUFFETT VALUE FRAMEWORK

### 1. Circle of Competence

**Assessment: Outside — Analysis Proceeds for Institutional Framework Exercise Only**

CMX.AX is a pre-revenue mineral exploration company listed on the ASX. From all observable data — zero revenue, deeply negative operating margins, 1.29 billion shares outstanding, a share price locked at AUD 0.030 for the entire 52-week range, and a market capitalisation of approximately AUD 38.7 million — this is a classic speculative exploration-stage entity whose "value" is entirely contingent on geological discovery.

Warren Buffett's Circle of Competence test is binary: can you predict with reasonable confidence what this business will earn in five years, or in ten? For a mineral explorer, the honest answer is no. The proposition rests entirely on two deeply uncertain variables — (1) whether economically viable deposits exist beneath the company's tenements, and (2) whether commodity prices will be adequate at the time of eventual production. Neither can be assessed with the probability-weighted precision that value investing demands. The required expertise is petrology and resource estimation, not business analysis.

Buffett has been explicit on this: he would not buy a junior miner or exploration company, not because such assets lack value, but because that value is geological speculation rather than franchise analysis. A practitioner of this framework should stop here.

The full 17-section analysis that follows is completed for institutional framework completeness and to document all observable risks. It does not alter the verdict.

### 2. Key Assumptions the Thesis Depends On

For any speculative bull case to materialise, all of the following must hold simultaneously:

1. **Mineralogical merit**: CMX holds tenements that contain economically extractable deposits of meaningful grade and size. Historically, only a small fraction of exploration tenements ever yield a commercial discovery; the base rate for any single programme is low.
2. **Commodity price support**: Prices for the target mineral(s) must remain at or above incentive levels by the time CMX could plausibly enter production — likely a decade away at minimum under the best scenario.
3. **Capital access without fatal dilution**: The company must raise further equity or attract a joint-venture partner to fund exploration and development, without diluting existing shareholders to near zero. With 1.29 billion shares already on issue for a company generating no revenue, the dilution runway is well-worn.
4. **Management execution**: Geological programmes must be run competently, permit conditions met, and environmental obligations satisfied. Regulatory approvals for Australian exploration projects routinely run years.
5. **Market re-rating catalyst**: For current shareholders to realise a return at today's AUD 0.030, the market must re-rate the company materially upward — requiring at minimum a positive drill intercept, a JORC resource announcement, or a corporate transaction. Without such a catalyst, the price is held in place by thin trading volume rather than fundamental support.

None of these assumptions is verifiable from the data provided. The investment is almost entirely binary: a discovery happens or it does not.
