## PART A — BUFFETT VALUE FRAMEWORK

### 1. Circle of Competence

Gold mining sits at the **boundary** of the circle of competence. The mechanics of the business are understandable — extract ore, process it, sell gold at spot prices, manage costs — but investment returns are driven almost entirely by the gold price, which is determined by forces (global monetary policy, central bank demand, geopolitical risk aversion, US dollar strength) that resist reliable long-term forecasting. Buffett himself has long avoided gold as an asset class, noting that it has no earnings power of its own and that its value is derived entirely from what the next buyer will pay.

Newmont is fundamentally a price-taker on its product. The analytical question is not "what is gold worth?" but rather "at what gold price does Newmont's current valuation make sense, and how likely is that price to persist?" This is workable analysis. Proceed with heightened uncertainty and a wider margin of safety requirement than I would apply to a consumer staple or financial services business.

### 2. Key Assumptions (5)

1. **Gold sustains above USD 2,500/oz** — the 2025 results are underpinned by an elevated gold price. If gold mean-reverts toward USD 1,800-2,000, earnings could fall 40-60% from current levels.
2. **CEO succession is seamless** — Natascha Viljoen (President & COO) replaced retiring Tom Palmer (announced September 2025). She brings deep operational familiarity but faces the challenge of defining her own strategic agenda following transformational years of M&A.
3. **Newcrest integration delivers its synergy targets** — the acquisition closed late 2023 and drove the step-change in 2025 FCF. Sustained cost and operational synergies are baked into forward earnings; any integration slippage reverses the thesis.
4. **Ahafo North reaches commercial production and nameplate throughput** — first gold was poured in September 2025; the ramp to commercial production was targeted for Q4 2025. This new low-cost Ghanaian production underpins reserve life extension.
5. **Geopolitical stability at key operations** — Ghana (Ahafo, Akyem), Peru, Australia (Boddington), and Nevada collectively represent the majority of production. Any sustained operational disruption would materially impair guidance.

### 3. Business Quality & Moat

Newmont is the world's largest gold producer by output and reserve life, with a globally diversified portfolio spanning six continents. Scale provides genuine operating advantages: access to lower-cost capital, overhead leverage across more ounces, and world-class safety and environmental capabilities that smaller operators cannot match economically. However, gold miners are fundamentally commodity businesses — Newmont sells an identical product at the identical spot price to every other producer. There is no pricing power, no switching cost, no network effect, and no customer loyalty.

**Moat assessment:**
- **Type:** Scale economies (overhead leverage) + resource quality (tier-1 long-life assets, 20-year-plus reserve profile)
- **Strength: Narrow** at the operating level (against smaller peers); **None** in Morningstar's sense (no structural protection against commodity price cycles)
- **Trend:** Stable post-Newcrest integration; Ahafo North adds incremental low-cost ounces
- **Five-year durability:** Newmont's scale advantage is durable as long as the gold price remains above its all-in sustaining cost. The diversified portfolio reduces single-asset risk. However, no structural barrier prevents the gold price from falling sharply, which would devastate margins regardless of operational quality. The Morningstar "None" moat rating is defensible and I apply "Narrow" only to reflect real — but non-durable — competitive advantages at the operating level.
