### 1. Circle of Competence

**Inside, with caveats.** CSL's core economics are simple to state even if the science is not: CSL Behring collects blood plasma at scale and fractionates it into life-sustaining therapies (immunoglobulins, albumin, clotting factors) for patients with no substitute; CSL Seqirus is one of the world's largest influenza vaccine makers; CSL Vifor sells iron-deficiency and nephrology therapies.
