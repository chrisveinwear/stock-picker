## PART A — BUFFETT VALUE FRAMEWORK

### 1. Circle of Competence

GOLD.AX is the ETFS Physical Gold exchange-traded product listed on the Australian Securities Exchange, managed by ETFS Management (AUS) Limited (a WisdomTree company). It is not an operating business. Each unit represents a fractional entitlement to a quantity of physical gold bullion held in allocated vault storage by a custodian. The product earns no revenue, employs no meaningful workforce, deploys no competitive strategy, and produces no earnings. Its unit price tracks the AUD-denominated spot gold price multiplied by the troy-ounce entitlement per unit, less the management expense ratio (~0.40% p.a.) accrued daily.

This instrument falls **outside** Buffett's circle of competence as defined for equity investment. Buffett has been consistently explicit over fifty years: gold is an unproductive asset. It does not grow, does not innovate, and generates no owner earnings to capitalise. The "intrinsic value" of a gold ETF cannot be derived from discounted cash flows; it is the market value of the gold it holds, itself determined by sentiment, geopolitics, inflation expectations, central bank demand, and the USD/AUD exchange rate — not by the business's capacity to compound capital.

**This analysis proceeds with a modified framework throughout.** Buffett equity criteria are applied where meaningful and explicitly flagged as not applicable elsewhere. The core valuation methodology in Section 6 anchors to gold price fundamentals (incentive price, AUD/USD sensitivity) rather than a conventional earnings-based DCF. The instrument remains analytically interesting — it is a legitimate portfolio hedge and store-of-value vehicle — but it is not a Buffett equity.
