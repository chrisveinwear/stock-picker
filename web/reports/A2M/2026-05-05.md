## PART A — BUFFETT VALUE FRAMEWORK

### 1. Circle of Competence

**Inside the circle.** a2 Milk is a consumer branded food company that licences proprietary genetics to dairy farmers to produce milk containing only the A2 beta-casein protein (not A1). The core business is understandable: collect a brand premium on a biologically differentiated product, primarily through infant formula sold in Australia/NZ and into China. Revenue comes from selling branded product at a significant premium to generic formula — the same model as any premium FMCG brand. The China daigou channel (cross-border ecommerce) adds complexity but is trackable. The US expansion adds execution risk, demonstrated vividly today.

---

### 2. Key Assumptions (verify quarterly)

1. **The a2 protein science holds** — the company's brand rests on consumer belief that A2-only milk is digestibly superior. If a major peer-reviewed study debunks this, the premium collapses.
2. **China infant formula demand recovers** — China's birth rate decline is structural. a2 Milk's China thesis requires the premium English-label segment to remain robust even as total births fall.
3. **Daigou channel stays legal and functional** — cross-border ecommerce regulations in China periodically tighten; any crackdown would meaningfully hurt revenue.
4. **US recall is contained and reputational damage is limited** — today's recall is the thesis-test event. If US consumers or retailers permanently de-list a2 Platinum, the US growth leg disappears.
5. **FX stability** — a2 Milk earns largely in NZD and recognises in AUD. A sustained NZD weakening vs AUD would flatter reported AUD numbers; a reversal hurts.

---

### 3. Business Quality & Moat

- **Moat type:** Intangible assets (brand + IP) with emerging cost advantage via proprietary herd genetics
- **Strength:** Narrow-to-moderate
- **Trend: Stable** (recovering post-COVID daigou disruption, but US recall is a fresh narrowing signal)

**Brand moat:** In its key markets (ANZ infant formula, Chinese English-label formula), a2 Platinum commands a clear price premium — 30–50% above most competitors. Parents form deep brand loyalty around infant formula; once a brand is established in early feeding, switching rates are extremely low. This is the best kind of consumer franchise.

**IP moat:** The a2 Corporation holds patents over the process of identifying and breeding A2-only cows. While the core patent protections have weakened over time, the herd genetics know-how, supply chain certification, and testing infrastructure create meaningful barriers to quick replication. Competitors can claim "A2-type" milk but cannot credibly certify purity at scale with the same credibility.

**Why not wide?** a2 Milk's moat is constrained by: (1) a relatively small addressable market (premium infant formula vs. total dairy), (2) fierce competition from Nestlé, Danone, Reckitt, and Chinese national champions who have deep distribution and government relationships, and (3) execution risk in the US where brand recall is genuinely unproven.

**Durability test:** In 10 years, will the a2 brand still command a premium in ANZ and China? Almost certainly yes in ANZ. Probable in China if geopolitical relations hold. US remains unproven. The brand is real; the question is scale of the opportunity.

The US expansion is an organic bet on distribution rather than an expensive M&A move.
